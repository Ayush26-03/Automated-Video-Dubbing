About DubStudio AI

DubStudio AI transforms videos into 25+ global languages with neural voice synthesis, temporal speech synchronization, and automated subtitles.

Speech-to-Text Faster-Whisper (CTranslate2) generates timestamped multilingual transcriptions.
Neural Translation Meta NLLB-200 with repetition-penalized beam search.
Neural Voice Dubbing Edge-TTS high-fidelity neural voice synthesis.
Temporal Audio Sync FFmpeg atempo dynamic cadence stretching for lip & gesture sync.
